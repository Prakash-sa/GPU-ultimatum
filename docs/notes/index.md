### Foundations: why GPUs are built this way

Parts 1–15 come from [Before GPU Engineering](before-gpu/index.md), a 56-chapter notebook by
Ranjan Yadav (MIT): transistors and memory buses first, then CUDA's execution model, the
memory wall, registers and occupancy, coalescing, tiling, and profiling. They sit in the
sidebar under the numbered notes. If GPUs are new to you, read them first; otherwise use
them to go deeper on a topic.

!!! tip "The one rule"
    Most scientific kernels (stencils, LBM, SpMV, statevector updates) are
    **memory-bandwidth bound**. Before optimizing arithmetic, count bytes moved per
    step and compare against the card's peak bandwidth
    (see [spec sheets](../gpu-spec-sheets.md)).
